Mended Stoic

Ancient Stoic wisdom for modern life — Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus and Seneca.

Mended Stoic is a YouTube channel of calm, practical lessons from Stoic philosophy. Every video is built around quotes taken verbatim from public-domain translations of the classical texts, with an original perspective on how to apply them today.

Narration and images are generated with AI tools, and every upload is disclosed as such on YouTube.
