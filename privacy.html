Privacy Policy

Mended Stoic uploader · Last updated: November 15, 2026

What the app is

Mended Stoic uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel @MendedStoic. Its only purpose is to upload, schedule and update videos and thumbnails on that channel using the YouTube Data API.

Data it accesses

The app requests access only to the owner's own YouTube channel (scopes youtube.upload and youtube). It does not access, collect or store data from any other person, and it does not read viewer information, comments or analytics.

How data is stored

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's private server, readable by a single system user, and is used solely to publish the channel's own videos. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the channel's About page on YouTube.
